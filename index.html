What to check before ordering

Confirm the seller and variant

Open the original marketplace listing and check that the item, seller, color and size match the catalog row. Confirm that your agent accepts the original link before paying.

Compare measurements and QC photos

For clothing, compare flat measurements against a garment you own. If your agent offers warehouse photos, check the size label, color, seams and any visible damage before approving shipment.

Compare the full cost

Ask which domestic delivery, service and payment charges are already included. Compare parcel quotes using both actual and volumetric weight, then allow for any taxes or duties.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference for people comparing product links before ordering through a purchasing agent. A useful row identifies the item, shows a reference price and helps you locate the original seller listing.

Can I use an item in this catalog with HooBuy?

Find the item on HipoBuyFind using the title and item ID. If the listing provides the original marketplace URL, check that URL with HooBuy and confirm the variant before ordering.

What does QC mean when shopping with an agent?

QC means quality control. If the agent provides photos after the item reaches its warehouse, use them to compare the received variant and visible condition with your order.

Why is the final cost different from the spreadsheet price?

The listed price refers to the product. Domestic delivery, agent fees, currency conversion, international shipping and applicable import charges can change the amount you pay.
